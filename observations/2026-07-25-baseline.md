## RGT · Rights and access

### The public-records instrument

Kansas Open Records Act is **K.S.A. 45-215 et seq.**, requiring a response within **three business days**, with **$0.25 per page** deemed a reasonable copy fee. `R`

Kansas Open Meetings Act is **K.S.A. 75-4317 et seq.** KOMA contains **no requirement to publish an agenda, no requirement to keep minutes, and no requirement to post advance notice** to the general public — notice is owed only to those who individually request it. `R`

That second finding is the significant one and is entered as a standing condition rather than an event. **The openness of a Kansas public meeting is opt-in for the citizen and not opt-out for the body.** A resident who does not know to ask is owed nothing. Lenexa exceeds this floor in practice by publishing agendas, packets, and video, which is a credit to the city and is recorded as such — but it is a municipal choice, not a statutory guarantee, and it could be withdrawn without violating state law.

### Absence of a comprehensive privacy instrument

Kansas has **no comprehensive consumer data privacy act.** `R` — recorded as an explicit absence per canon §4.3, not as an unchecked field.

Kansas has **no statute governing automated licence plate readers.** `R` — likewise an explicit absence. ALPR deployment in this jurisdiction proceeds under no state-level statutory authority, constraint, retention limit, or audit requirement.

Kansas app-store age-verification bill **SB 372** is recorded as **"Died"** in the 2025–26 session. `R`

### Absence of investigative civilian oversight

There is **no civilian review board with investigative power** in Lenexa or in Johnson County, Kansas. `R`

The **Lenexa Police Community Advisory Board** exists, established by unanimous Council vote on 2021-12-07, seven members on staggered terms, a majority required to live or work in Lenexa, advising on biased-based policing, use of force, mental health response, and community outreach, meeting in public with minutes available. `R`

It is advisory. The distinction is recorded neutrally and precisely because it matters to every other observation in this file: **the body that exists reviews and advises; it does not investigate.**

## RGT + CMN · Surveillance capability against oversight capability

This pairing is the central finding of the genesis snapshot, and it is a finding about **proportion**, not about conduct.

| Condition | State |
|---|---|
| ALPR devices in Johnson County KS | **475** — 26.1% of the Kansas state total, ranking **1st of 83 counties** `R` |
| Lenexa drone-as-first-responder fleet | **7 in service**, with **$995,335** approved for six more `R` |
| Kansas statute governing ALPR use, retention, or audit | **None** `R` |
| Civilian body empowered to investigate a complaint | **None** `R` |
| Advisory body | Police Community Advisory Board `R` |

A documented instance exists in which a **city critic accumulated roughly 150 plate reads in under two years** through ALPR infrastructure. `R`

That single observation is the reason the privacy invariant in canon §2 is written as an absolute rather than a preference. A jurisdiction that leads its state in plate-reader density, operates an expanding drone fleet, has no governing statute, and has no investigative oversight body is a jurisdiction where **a civic monitoring record that held person-level data would itself become part of the problem it was built to describe.** ARK-CIVIC holds none, by design, permanently.

## SAF · Safety capability

**Lenexa publishes a live Calls for Service feed**, publicly reachable with no login, in which **yellow rows indicate a drone was dispatched.** `R`

This is recorded as a genuine transparency capability, and it is unusual. Very few municipal departments of this size publish live dispatch with an explicit unmanned-aerial indicator. It is the strongest single artifact of good faith in this jurisdiction's public-safety data posture and is entered as such.

**Lenexa City Council** meets 1st and 3rd Tuesday at 19:00, Committee of the Whole 2nd and 4th Tuesday, with full packets published as PDF and video archived on Swagit since 2023-01-03. `R`

**FBI Crime Data Explorer** participation for Lenexa PD is **unresolved** — API access rate-limited on a shared demonstration key. `I` Requires a personal `api.data.gov` key to confirm. Entered as an open item rather than as either a positive or a negative finding, per canon §4.3.

## CMN · Commons access

Findings in this register describe **arrangements and instruments**. Per canon §3.1 they name no individual and no professional community, and each requires two independent sources before it stands.

### Machine-readable public data that is genuinely reachable

- **MARC regional open data** — ArcGIS Hub with a working DCAT-US catalogue feed listing **670 datasets**. `R` Free.
- **Johnson County AIMS ArcGIS Server** — live, version 11.5, with approximately **90 free datasets**. `R`
- **Lenexa live Calls for Service** — free, no login. `R`
- **FBI Crime Data Explorer API** — free, key required. `R`

The commons in this jurisdiction is **substantially better provisioned than average**, and that is recorded first and without qualification. The findings below are gaps in an otherwise strong posture, not an indictment of it.

### Access barriers recorded as findings

- **Johnson County AIMS bulk data is paid.** Approximately 90 datasets are free; bulk acquisition proceeds through a paid Digital Data Request. `R` The condition is recorded neutrally: the marginal cost of duplicating a GIS dataset is near zero, and a fee at that boundary functions as a filter on who can hold a full copy of county spatial data.
- **Lenexa municipal code carries no machine-readable feed.** The codifier is enCodePlus; the interactive viewer is closed to automated retrieval by robots policy while the export endpoint remains retrievable. The code was last codified in 2008 pursuant to K.S.A. 12-3014 and 12-3015. `R`
- **The Lenexa Police Community Advisory Board landing page returns "Access Denied" to automated retrieval**, while its agenda PDFs remain reachable. `R` The oversight body's own front page is the least machine-reachable public-safety page in the city's estate. Recorded as an observed condition with no assertion of intent — a robots or WAF rule is the ordinary explanation.
- **Kansas has no official legislative bill-status API.** A bill-tracking RFP closed 2026-07-31. `R`
- **No Johnson County election-results API or CSV archive.** `R`
- **No single State of Kansas open data portal.** `R`
- **No Lenexa RSS feed.** Agenda notification is e-mail-list only. `R`

### The shape these gaps make

Each item above is individually minor and individually defensible. Taken together they describe a consistent condition: **in this jurisdiction, the data describing government is markedly less machine-reachable than the data describing land, roads, and geography.**

Spatial data has an open server. Legislation, ordinances, election results, and oversight proceedings do not. The asymmetry is almost certainly an artifact of which systems were procured when and by whom, rather than any decision to restrict — but the effect on a resident, a parish, or a small agency attempting to track the civic condition is the same regardless of how it arose, and the effect is what ARK records.
