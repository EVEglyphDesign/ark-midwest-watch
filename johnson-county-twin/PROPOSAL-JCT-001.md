## The offer in one line

A finished Johnson County Commons Twin, delivered as a single sealed appliance, for a
fixed **$20,000**, handed to Jeff Eden to take forward commercially if he wants it.

## What is in the box

- One compact inference/serving appliance, Strix Halo class — Ryzen AI 9 HX 470 tier,
  32 GB, ~150 × 150 × 43 mm, under 120 W, runs 24/7 on hotel or office power, reachable
  over Tailscale. Not a workstation, not a rack. (Hardware tier and pricing established in
  the 2026-07-28 hardware review: 32 GB class ~$1,359; 128 GB Strix Halo now $3,000–3,300
  because of the memory shortage, and not needed for this workload.)
- A full local mirror of the ARK Midwest Watch civic holdings for Lenexa and Johnson
  County — the corrected baseline of **2,756,484 held records** and **89 partial layers**,
  held on disk, not linked. Person-level datasets are indexed as existing and never
  requested or mirrored.
- Every reachable dataset mapped into one **3D spatial surface** of the county, so parcels,
  infrastructure, streets and places sit in the same coordinate space instead of in
  fourteen incompatible viewers.
- An **anonymised presence layer**: existing civic, commercial and residential camera
  systems keep operating exactly as they do, with a filter added on top that emits counts
  and movement only. No identity, no faces, no live video feed — live all the same.
- **Citizen view**: residents open a phone, see movement and traffic patterns in their
  zone, and can upload a photo which returns only how many people are there.
- **Sharing parity**: every sharing function people already expect from Facebook, available
  natively — invite, share a place, go together.
- **No pre-cognitive loading.** No promotional feed before arrival. Nothing is marketed at
  the front. Once you are physically there — once you have already picked the restaurant —
  the surface adds what is good here, what to order, what not to miss. It is a layer on the
  experience, added where nobody is doing it, so no merchant and no resident has a
  complaint to make.
- **Responsible payment at the point of experience** — pay in place, less time at the till.
  Delivery cost of the payment path is effectively nil, so the proceeds route to a named
  **local Johnson County charity** rather than to a rake.
- **Concentric safety beacons**, three zones, resident-set: a spoken safe word raises a
  beacon to other users, camera feeds inside the raised zone are digitally stamped, and a
  stamped feed is retrievable **only on a specific subpoena**. The evidence stays with the
  citizens and out of a central pool.

## Governing principles carried into the box

- Safety first, betterment second.
- Conditions, never people. Findings file against contracts, formats, fees and access
  arrangements — never against employees or residents.
- Hold rather than merely link: an upstream shutdown must not erase the evidence surface.
- Front end, not replacement. Google, Yelp, Nextdoor, OpenStreetMap and city feeds remain
  usable back ends.
- No account, no exit cost. Nothing is held hostage when a visitor leaves the geofence.
- No aggregate signal auto-notifies the Lenexa Police Department or any other authority.
