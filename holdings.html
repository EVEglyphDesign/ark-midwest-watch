2,870,381 records across 827 files are held in this repository, pulled from each publisher's own endpoint. A wall that is named is a finding; a wall that is silently skipped is a lie by omission — so every dataset that could not be pulled is listed below with the reason and with who can remove it.

79 datasets were reachable and deliberately not requested.

The person-level screen runs before any content request is made, so these were never fetched at all — not fetched and discarded. That includes the City of Lenexa Calls for Service service. This is the one wall in the list that this record imposes on itself, and it is the reason the honest answer to what did you take is: nothing about any person.

Structural lock-ins

Not per-dataset failures — conditions of the jurisdiction. Each is a CMN register finding, filed against the arrangement and never against the people who operate it.

CMN-1 · No county open-data feed

Eight plausible catalogue endpoints probed, none exist. County data is reachable only by knowing an ArcGIS REST directory exists and how to walk it. MARC and the State of Kansas both publish DCAT-US 1.1; the county does not.

removable by the county — the platform emits this feed as a configuration option

CMN-2 · Bulk data is a paid request

Roughly 90 datasets download free; beyond that is a priced Digital Data Request. Data produced by public taxation is metered on re-access.

removable by fee-schedule decision

CMN-3 · Land data is reachable, government data is not

Parcels, aquifers, and soils resolve as queryable services. Budgets, contracts, meeting records, and procurement appear as machine-readable distributions nowhere in the 1,755 records indexed. Not deliberate — GIS platforms emit APIs by default and document systems do not.

removable by publishing documents through any system with a feed

CMN-4 · The open-meetings floor is opt-in

KOMA requires no agenda, no minutes, and no posted notice. Where a Kansas public body publishes these, it is a local choice a future body can reverse without violating any statute.

removable only by the Legislature
