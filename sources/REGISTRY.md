### 17. Kansas Open Records Act (KORA)

- **Statute citation:** **K.S.A. 45-215 through 45-223** — "K.S.A. 45-215 through 45-223 shall be known and may be cited as the open records act" ([Kansas Revisor, 45-215](https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch45/045_002_0015.html))
- **Response-time requirement:** an agency must act on a request "as soon as possible, but no later than the end of the third business day following the date the request was received," excluding weekends and holidays; if access or copies are not provided within that window the agency must give a detailed explanation for the delay, and on request a written statement of the legal grounds for any denial ([Kansas AG KORA FAQ](https://www.ag.ks.gov/divisions/administration/open-government/kora-faq))
- **Fees:** KORA sets no specific fee; agencies may charge "reasonable fees" not exceeding actual costs (staff search/copy/redaction time, computer services), but not overhead, capital improvements, utilities or rent. Pre-payment of estimated costs may be required with refunds for overpayment. For executive-branch state agencies, K.S.A. 45-219(c) allows appeal of fee reasonableness to the Secretary of Administration, and "a fee equal to or less than $.25 per page shall be deemed a reasonable fee." There is no poverty exception ([Kansas AG KORA FAQ](https://www.ag.ks.gov/divisions/administration/open-government/kora-faq))
- **Policy and burden:** records "shall be open for inspection by any person unless otherwise provided" and the act is to be liberally construed (K.S.A. 45-216(a)); the burden of proving an exemption rests on the agency withholding ([Kansas AG KORA FAQ](https://www.ag.ks.gov/divisions/administration/open-government/kora-faq))
- **Enforcement:** by the Attorney General, a county or district attorney, or private citizens, in district court where the records are located; remedies include turnover, attorney fees and fines up to **$500 per violation** in AG/DA-brought cases; there are no criminal penalties. Complaints to the AG must be on the prescribed form and under penalty of perjury (K.S.A. 45-252), and the AG refers complaints against local government to the county/district attorney ([Kansas AG KORA FAQ](https://www.ag.ks.gov/divisions/administration/open-government/kora-faq)); complaint intake at [KOMA-KORA Violations](https://www.ag.ks.gov/file-a-complaint/koma-kora-violations); further material at [AG Open Government Resources](https://www.ag.ks.gov/divisions/administration/open-government/resources)
- **Local application:** Lenexa's request channels are [Request a Public Record](https://www.lenexa.com/Government/Codes-Records-Policies/Request-a-Public-Record) and, for police records, [Police KORA requests](https://www.lenexa.com/City-Services/Public-Safety/Police-Services/Police-Records/Request-Police-Kansas-Open-Records)
- **Machine-readable feed:** **No**
- **Access barrier:** None — free, no login

### 18. Kansas Open Meetings Act (KOMA)

- **Statute citation:** **K.S.A. 75-4317 through 75-4320a** — subsection (c) states "K.S.A. 75-4317 through 75-4320a shall be known and may be cited as the open meetings act"; subsection (a) declares the policy that "meetings for the conduct of governmental affairs and the transaction of governmental business be open to the public" ([Kansas Revisor, 75-4317](https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch75/075_043_0017.html))
- **Official guidance page:** [Kansas AG KOMA FAQ](https://www.ag.ks.gov/divisions/administration/open-government/koma-faq)
- **Key requirements (from the AG guidance):** a KOMA "meeting" requires (1) an interactive discussion (2) by and between at least a majority of the body (3) on matters relating to its functions; "majority" is the next whole number greater than half. Binding action must be taken openly. Executive sessions require an open meeting first, a topic listed in **K.S.A. 75-4319(b)** (the FAQ states there are currently 14 listed topics), and a motion — recorded in the minutes — containing a justification for closure, the subject, and the time and place the open meeting will resume. Cameras and recorders may not be prohibited. **KOMA does not require an agenda, does not require minutes beyond executive-session motions, does not require public comment, and does not require posted or published notice** — notice of time, date and place must be given only to those who request it, and requests may be set to expire annually with advance warning ([Kansas AG KOMA FAQ](https://www.ag.ks.gov/divisions/administration/open-government/koma-faq))
- **Lawsuit authority:** K.S.A. 75-4320a(a) ([Kansas AG KOMA FAQ](https://www.ag.ks.gov/divisions/administration/open-government/koma-faq)); complaints via [KOMA-KORA Violations](https://www.ag.ks.gov/file-a-complaint/koma-kora-violations)
- **Machine-readable feed:** **No**
- **Access barrier:** None — free, no login

### 21. Kansas Student Data Privacy Act

- **Statute citation:** **K.S.A. 72-6312 through 72-6320** — "K.S.A. 72-6312 through 72-6320, and amendments thereto, shall be known and may be cited as the student data privacy act" ([Kansas Legislature statute page](https://www.kslegislature.gov/li/b2025_26/statute/072_000_0000_chapter/072_063_0000_article/072_063_0012_section/072_063_0012_k/)); chapter listing with section titles at [Kansas Revisor, Chapter 72](https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/ksa_ch72.html) (72-6312 citation, 72-6313 definitions, 72-6314 disclosure, **72-6315 collection of biometric data prohibited**, 72-6316 nonacademic tests/surveys, 72-6317 enforcement, 72-6318 breach notification, 72-6319 publication of data categories, 72-6320 annual report to Governor and Legislature)
- **Scope:** the Act prohibits unauthorized disclosure of student data and personally identifiable student data, unauthorized collection of biometric data from a student, and unauthorized use of any device or mechanism to assess a student's psychological or emotional state; it requires notification to affected students of breaches or unauthorized disclosure; and it bars tests, questionnaires, surveys or examinations asking about a student's or parent's beliefs or practices on sex, family life, morality or religion without written parental notice and permission ([Kansas AG — Student Data Privacy](https://www.ag.ks.gov/file-a-complaint/student-data-privacy))
- **Disclosure mechanics:** disclosure to other state agencies or service providers requires a data-sharing agreement specifying purpose, scope and duration, restricting use, imposing access/security restrictions, and requiring destruction to **NIST SP 800-88** standards when no longer needed; disclosures to other governmental entities or research organizations must be aggregate-only unless the student or guardian consents in writing ([K.S.A. 72-6314 via FindLaw](https://codes.findlaw.com/ks/chapter-72-schools/ks-st-sect-72-6314/))
- **Biometric provision text:** [K.S.A. 72-6315](https://ksrevisor.gov/statutes/chapters/ch72/072_063_0015.html) (L. 2014, ch. 124, § 4; prior law 72-6218)
- **Enforcement:** the Attorney General or a district attorney may sue and seek injunctive relief under K.S.A. 72-6317; only Douglas, **Johnson**, Reno, Sedgwick, Shawnee and Wyandotte counties have district attorneys, so Johnson County residents may complain to either the DA or the Office of the Attorney General; residents of all counties may complain to the OAG ([Kansas AG — Student Data Privacy](https://www.ag.ks.gov/file-a-complaint/student-data-privacy))
